Welcome to Casa Savasana

On the edge of the lowland jungle just above the Pacific Coast, Casa Savasana is the perfect getaway for solo travelers and couples coming to Mazunte for the sun, surf, beaches and yoga! Relax overlooking our jungle garden where you can see many birds and perhaps an iguana or two.

We are located 4km (10 minutes) away from Mazunte.

Location

Mazunte, Oaxaca
